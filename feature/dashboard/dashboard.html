Location

    For the weather screens. Your browser looks the place up; the panel only keeps its coordinates. Weather comes from Open-Meteo every 15 minutes.

    World clocks

        Up to four. Names up to 10 characters. Daylight saving comes from your browser's time zone database; the panel then keeps time by itself. With no clocks the screen is skipped.

        Night mode

        The whole panel sleeps between these times, whatever pattern is running. Any knob wakes it; at night it goes back to sleep after 10 minutes.

        Orientation

        Calibrate

        Hang the panel upright (portrait) and press This is upright. If the dashboard then reads upside down in portrait or landscape, press the matching button. Without a sensor, turn K2 on the dashboard to rotate by hand. Leave the first switch off when nothing is connected: the two pins are also the board's serial console.

        GIFs

        The panel's storage is not formatted yet, so GIFs cannot be saved. Format it once on the Patterns page.

        On the panel

          A GIF plays after every dashboard screen: at least one loop, at most 20 seconds. The panel keeps a portrait and a landscape version.
